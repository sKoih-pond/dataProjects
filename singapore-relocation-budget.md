## 4. Minimum salary floor

### Scenario A — you work in SG as a citizen/PR (CPF applies)
Employee CPF deduction is **20%** of gross (up to the S$8,000/month ceiling from 1 Jan 2026), so take-home ≈ 80% of gross ([CPF contribution rates 2026 — Endowus](https://endowus.com/insights/how-much-cpf-contribution-rates), [Growbeansprout](https://growbeansprout.com/cpf-contribution-changes-jan-2026)). Income tax is assessed the following year and is tiny at these levels — first S$20,000 of chargeable income is tax-free ([IRAS resident rates](https://www.iras.gov.sg/taxes/individual-income-tax/basics-of-individual-income-tax/tax-residency-and-tax-rates/individual-income-tax-rates)); effective rate ≈ 0–2% for a S$36–50k year.

| | Gross needed | Take-home (×0.8) | Covers |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Absolute floor (lean)** | **S$2,900 – 3,000/mo** | ~S$2,350 | S$1,850 spend + ~S$500 savings |
| **Recommended floor (comfortable + 15% savings)** | **S$4,000 – 4,200/mo** | ~S$3,250 | S$2,750 spend + ~S$500 savings |

IT support at S$2,700–3,500 straddles the absolute floor — livable lean, tight for savings. Entry data analyst or network roles (S$3,900+) clear the recommended floor.

### Scenario B — you work in SG as a foreigner (work pass sets the real floor)
No CPF deduction; tax as above once resident (≥183 days). The binding constraint is the pass ([MOM EP eligibility](https://www.mom.gov.sg/passes-and-permits/employment-pass/eligibility), [MOM S Pass eligibility](https://www.mom.gov.sg/passes-and-permits/s-pass/eligibility)):

- **S Pass:** minimum **S$3,300/mo** in 2026 (rising to S$3,600 for new applications from 1 Jan 2027) — quota- and levy-bound, so employers ration these.
- **Employment Pass:** minimum **S$5,600/mo** (S$6,200 financial services) plus COMPASS points.

Practical read: typical IT-support pay (S$2,700–3,500) sits **below or barely at** the S Pass line, so as a foreigner target data analyst / network roles at **≥S$3,300 (S Pass)**, or **≥S$5,600 (EP)** for a clean pass. Your AUD 80k preference (≈ S$5,730/mo) lands almost exactly at the EP threshold — a useful anchor, but expect entry data/IT roles in SG to offer less than that.

## 5. Bottom line

- **Monthly spend forecast:** ~**S$1,850** lean / ~**S$2,750** comfortable, room-rental setup.
- **Salary floor:** **S$3,000/mo** absolute minimum (local, lean), **S$4,000–4,200/mo** recommended; **S$3,300/mo** hard legal floor on S Pass, **S$5,600/mo** on EP if relocating as a foreigner.
- **Cash to land:** S$4,000–5,000 up front, then build a ~S$5,500 emergency fund.
- **Strategy note:** entry IT support pays at/below your floors — use it as a wedge only if hired locally; data analyst and network roles clear the recommended floor and (for foreigners) the pass thresholds. Your airline-satisfaction analysis project in this repo is exactly the kind of portfolio evidence SG data-role hiring screens for.
